Background

THE STORY

Teja Reygle is a 17-year-old videographer, photographer, and editor originally from Floyd, Virginia. Growing up surrounded by the mountains and creative community of the New River Valley gave him a strong eye for natural light, storytelling, and the kind of quiet moments that make a shot worth keeping.

With over 5 years of editing experience, he developed a deep feel for pacing, music, and color that shapes every project he takes on. That foundation in post-production gives his shooting a deliberate edge — he frames for the edit, not just the moment. His photography and videography work spans brand work, events, nonprofit documentary, and personal projects.

He shoots on a Lumix S5 with a Sigma 28–70mm f/2.8 and edits in DaVinci Resolve, with a color pipeline built around V-Log and custom LUTs for a cinematic, film-adjacent look. The goal on every project is the same — make something that actually feels like something.

How I Work

APPROACH

Every project starts with listening. Before anything gets shot or edited, the most important thing is understanding what the client actually needs — not just a deliverable, but a feeling. What should someone walk away thinking after they watch this?

Travel is never a problem and never an excuse. If you're willing to bring the vision, the location doesn't matter — some of the best work comes from places you least expect.
